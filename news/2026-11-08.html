丹佛斯植物科学中心 Czymmek 团队用 Thermo Scientific Hydra 等离子聚焦离子束扫描电镜（氩离子铣削与透过透镜探测器成像交替）对两种丝状蓝藻鱼腥藻做整细胞冷冻体积电镜成像。对齐后的图像导入 Dragonfly 3D World 2024.1（Comet Technologies Canada Inc., Montreal）做分割：用 multi-slice pen 手动分割羧酶体、藻青素颗粒和细胞壁，再用 Segmentation Wizard 中预训练的 2.5D（3 切片）U-Net Depth 5 模型（2024.1 与 2025.1）分割类囊体/藻胆体和多聚磷酸盐颗粒；随后把结构转为 multi-ROI 测量体积，用线测量工具测量类囊体厚度与间距，并用 2D animation 与 Movie Maker 导出三维视频。A team led by Czymmek at the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center imaged whole cells of two filamentous Anabaena species by cryo-volume electron microscopy on a Thermo Scientific Hydra plasma FIB-SEM, alternating argon milling with through-lens detector imaging. Aligned stacks were imported into Dragonfly 3D World 2024.1 (Comet Technologies Canada Inc., Montreal): carboxysomes, cyanophycin granules and cell walls were segmented manually with the multi-slice pen, while thylakoids/phycobilisomes and polyphosphate granules were segmented with the pre-trained 2.5D (3-slice) U-Net Depth 5 model in Segmentation Wizard (2024.1 and 2025.1). Structures were converted to a multi-ROI for volume measurements, thylakoid thickness and spacing were taken with the line-measurement tool, and 3D videos were exported with 2D animation and Movie Maker.

广东工业大学赵璐清、周翠英与长安大学彭建兵等用工业 CT 研究含人工毫米级裂纹黄土试样的初始破坏形态。预处理后的 CT 图像在 Dragonfly（Comet Technologies Canada）中做三维重建；再用软件内高度封装的 2.5D U-Net 插件（5 层编码器–解码器、初始 64 个滤波器、三连续切片输入、64×64 补丁、Ors Dice Loss、Adadelta 优化、训练 100 步）分割黄土基质、裂纹和孔隙。Luqing Zhao and Cuiying Zhou at Guangdong University of Technology, with Jianbing Peng at Chang'an University and colleagues, used industrial CT to study the initial failure morphology of loess specimens containing artificial millimeter-scale cracks. 熊蜂嗅觉系统的性二态在不同物种间保守Sexual dimorphisms of the olfactory system are conserved across bumblebee species

iScience · 杜伦大学等iScience · Durham University and collaborators2026-10-28学术论文Research paperDOI: 10.1016/j.isci.2026.118037

杜伦大学等团队比较了 6 种欧洲熊蜂野外工蜂和雄蜂的大脑与触角。共聚焦成像的触角叶和全脑在 Dragonfly 3D World（Object Research Systems）中分割：用 ROI Painter 的 Freehand Region 手工勾画每个嗅小球的起止和中部，再以 Z 向插值补全，约 20 个嗅小球后合并为 Multi-ROI，全部完成后用布尔运算 Union 合并，并以 Labeled Class Count 和 Connected Components 分析计数嗅小球并测量体积；全脑体积则用阈值分割加形态学闭运算与平滑得到。结果显示这些物种间嗅觉系统的雌雄差异是保守的。A team led from Durham University compared brains and antennae of wild workers and males from six European Bombus species. Confocal stacks of antennal lobes and whole brains were segmented in Dragonfly 3D World (Object Research Systems): each glomerulus was outlined at its start, middle and end with the Freehand Region tool in ROI Painter, completed by Z-interpolation, merged into a Multi-ROI after about 20 glomeruli, then combined with a Boolean Union so glomeruli could be counted (Labeled Class Count) and measured (Connected Components). Whole-brain volume was obtained by thresholding plus morphological close and smooth. The sexual dimorphisms of the olfactory system turned out to be conserved across these species.
