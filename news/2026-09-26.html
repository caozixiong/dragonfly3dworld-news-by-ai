新泡泡糖珊瑚物种：研究团队用 Dragonfly 3D World 做 micro-XCT 卵母细胞分割，首次在八放珊瑚中发现甲壳素骨架New bubblegum coral species: Dragonfly 3D World used for micro-XCT oocyte segmentation, first chitin scaffold found in octocorals

Scientific Reports（Nature）Scientific Reports (Nature)2026-09-13学术论文Research paper

研究团队在纳斯卡海脊（670 米深）发现泡泡糖珊瑚新物种 Paragorgia quitinosa，并首次在八放珊瑚组织中确认甲壳素（chitin）是无轴骨骼群体的结构支架。团队把 micro-XCT 扫描的珊瑚碎片（Zeiss Xradia 620 Versa，5.05 μm 体素）导入 Dragonfly 3D World，对 10 个随机选取的虹吸水螅体 ROI 沿 z 轴手动勾画并插值分割卵母细胞，进而计算每个 ROI 的 Feret 直径。A new bubblegum coral species, Paragorgia quitinosa, collected at 670 m depth on the Nazca Ridge, provides the first analytical evidence of chitin as a structural scaffold in octocoral tissues. The team segmented oocytes in Dragonfly 3D World from micro-XCT scans (Zeiss Xradia 620 Versa, 5.05 μm voxels) by manually outlining and interpolating ROIs along z in 10 haphazardly selected siphonozoids, then calculated Feret diameters for each ROI.
