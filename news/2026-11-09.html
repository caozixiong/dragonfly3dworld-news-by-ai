胰腺组织与胰岛分割模型的同步辐射 X 射线数据集（人类非糖尿病与糖尿病）Synchrotron X-ray Data of non-diseased and diabetic Human Pancreas Tissue and Islet Segmentation Models

EMBL 汉堡、海德堡大学等团队公开了 32 人 55 个人胰腺组织芯的同步辐射 micro-CT 虚拟组织学数据集（650 nm 体素），胰岛标注用 Dragonfly 3D World v2025.1（Comet Technologies Canada）内置 SAM2 与标注工具完成，并在 Segmentation Wizard 训练 2.5D U-Net 胰岛分割模型，模型随数据一起发布、可在 Dragonfly 3D World 中复用。An open synchrotron micro-CT dataset of human pancreas tissue; islet annotations were made with SAM2 and annotation tools in Dragonfly 3D World v2025.1, and dataset-specific 2.5D U-Net models trained in its Segmentation Wizard are released for reuse in Dragonfly.
