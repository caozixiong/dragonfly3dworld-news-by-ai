Microwave JournalMicrowave Journal2026-09-28版本前瞻Release preview

Microwave Journal 从增材制造应用视角梳理了即将发布的 Dragonfly 3D World 2027 十大功能：基于 Meta SAM 的 AI 实例分割可一键分离粉末颗粒、孔隙与缺熔缺陷；亚体素多重网格（Subvoxel Multi-Mesh）实现可重复的孔隙定量；还有 GPU 加速的 Fast Split、Rotational Views 旋转切片、可以直接对标触针仪的表面粗糙度分析，以及 Report Builder 报告生成器和免费的 Session Viewer 共享工具。产品经理 Zixiong Cao 在文中点评了新版本从数据探索到报告的端到端体验改进。Microwave Journal reviews ten highlights of the upcoming Dragonfly 3D World 2027 from an additive-manufacturing perspective: SAM-based AI instance segmentation that separates powder particles, pores and lack-of-fusion defects in one click; Subvoxel Multi-Mesh for repeatable porosity quantification; plus GPU-accelerated Fast Split, Rotational Views, stylus-comparable surface roughness analysis, the Report Builder, and the free Session Viewer for sharing. Product Manager Zixiong Cao comments on the release's end-to-end experience improvements, from data exploration to reporting.
