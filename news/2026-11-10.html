木材–塑料复合材料（WPC）中纤维与基体相互作用的研究Investigations into the interaction between fibrous elements and matrix in wood–plastic composite (WPC)

卢布尔雅那大学知识库（硕士论文，Alexander Simić）University of Ljubljana Repository (M.Sc. thesis, Alexander Simić)2026-10-11学术论文Research paper

卢布尔雅那大学（与吕勒奥理工大学合作）的硕士论文用 Zeiss Xradia 620 Versa micro-CT 以 0.73 µm 体素对木塑复合材料成像，并用 Comet Technologies Canada 的 Dragonfly 软件分析纤维与基体的相互作用。A University of Ljubljana master's thesis (with Luleå University of Technology) imaged wood–plastic composites on a Zeiss Xradia 620 Versa micro-CT at 0.73 µm voxel size and analysed the fibre–matrix interaction with Comet Technologies Canada's Dragonfly software.
