准确判定脊椎动物粪化石的螺旋肠类型：摩洛哥 Kem Kem 白垩纪地层案例Accurately assigning coprolites to vertebrate spiral intestine types: A case study from the Kem Kem beds (Morocco, Cretaceous)

Fossil Record (Pensoft)Fossil Record (Pensoft)2026-11-16学术论文Research paper

对摩洛哥 Kem Kem 地层 187 件白垩纪脊椎动物粪化石做 micro-CT（RX Solutions 扫描仪），判定其螺旋肠类型；三维渲染和分割在 Dragonfly 2024.1 中完成。Micro-CT study (RX Solutions scanner) of 187 Cretaceous vertebrate coprolites from the Kem Kem beds, Morocco, to assign them to spiral-intestine types; 3D rendering and segmentation were done in Dragonfly 2024.1.
