MGP 缺失揭示小鼠气道中此前未被认识的黏膜钙化Matrix Gla Protein deficiency reveals a previously unrecognized mucosal calcification in the mouse airways

bioRxiv（预印本）bioRxiv (preprint)2026-11-10学术论文Research paper

IMoPA（南锡）和麦吉尔大学的研究者表征了 MGP 缺失小鼠的气管钙化；Zeiss Xradia Versa 520 的 micro-CT 体积数据在 Dragonfly 3D World 中完成配准与拼接，黏膜和软骨钙化用其中的 2.5D U-Net 分割（Comet Technologies Canada 提供学术许可）。Researchers at IMoPA (Nancy) and McGill University characterize tracheal calcification in MGP-deficient mice; Zeiss Xradia Versa 520 micro-CT volumes were registered and stitched in Dragonfly 3D World, and mucosal and cartilage calcification was segmented with a 2.5D U-Net in Dragonfly 3D World (academic license from Comet Technologies Canada).

基于深度学习流程的成人耳蜗螺旋神经节完整三维图谱A Complete Three-Dimensional Spiral Ganglion Neuron Map in a Human Adult using a Deep Learning Pipeline

bioRxiv（预印本）bioRxiv (preprint)2026-11-11学术论文Research paper

麻省眼耳医院的研究者根据 421 张连续 H&E 切片，绘制了首份完整的人耳蜗螺旋神经节三维图谱；螺旋神经节细胞核由在 Dragonfly 3D World 2024.1（Comet）中用 Deep Learning Tool 训练的自定义深度学习分割模型检测。Mass Eye and Ear researchers build the first complete 3D spiral ganglion neuron map of a human cochlea from 421 consecutive H&E sections; SGN nuclei were detected with a custom deep-learning segmentation model trained in Dragonfly 3D World 2024.1 (Comet) using its Deep Learning Tool.
